
Specifications for the Defuse Ragebait feature

User can override per domain in Off-List.

Detects outrage-bait and rage-inducing content (anger, name-calling, polarising hooks) designed to maximise engagement over accuracy.

Labels toxic or inflammatory chunks and offers gentler presentation (e.g. collapsed or muted styling) via nutrient labels; user remains in control.

Settings module: `defuseRagebait`.
