# better:net — Brand Guidelines

## Name & Identity

- **Name:** better:net
- **Domain:** better-net.com
- **Tagline:** Agent AI Browser for a better internet.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Navy (Primary) | `#1a3a5c` | Headers, primary buttons, headings |
| Energised Red | `#e63946` | Accents, CTAs, highlights |
| Mid Blue | `#457b9d` | Links, secondary accents |
| Dark | `#0d1b2a` | Footer background |
| Light Grey | `#f8f9fa` | Section backgrounds |

### Rationale
- **Navy** conveys trust and reliability for a user-first browser brand.
- **Red-orange accent** brings energy and momentum for an early-stage product.
- **Clean white/grey backgrounds** ensure legibility and a modern, professional feel.

## Typography

- **Font:** System UI stack (`Segoe UI`, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif)
- **Headings:** Bold (700–800 weight)
- **Body:** Regular (400), comfortable line-height (1.6–1.7)

## Voice & Tone

- **Trustworthy** — evidence-based, measured, credible
- **Energised** — action-oriented, urgent without being alarmist
- **Inclusive** — welcoming to mainstream audiences
- **Direct** — no jargon, plain English

## Core Idea

Better Net is a browser built for users, not advertisers, engagement algorithms, or manipulation systems.

The brand should feel:

* calm
* thoughtful
* trustworthy
* humane
* intelligent
* quietly rebellious

Not:

* corporate
* hyper-optimised
* revolutionary
* cyberpunk
* AI-hype
* dystopian

## Emotional Positioning

The modern internet increasingly feels:

* manipulative
* exhausting
* noisy
* hostile to attention
* engineered for engagement over understanding

Better Net offers:

* clarity
* focus
* user agency
* calm
* relief
* transparency

The emotional journey:

1. Recognition - often with anger or frustration (“yes, the web feels broken”)
2. Relief (“this feels calmer and more honest”)
3. Agency (“I am back in control”)

Tone of Voice

Write like:

* thoughtful engineers

* calm experts

Avoid:

* hype

* corporate jargon
* doom-mongering

* direct

* quietly confident

⸻

Visual Style

Overall Aesthetic

A blend of:

* editorial design
* thoughtful indie software
* calm productivity tools
* hand-crafted warmth

The product should feel like:
“a trustworthy workshop fixing the web.”

⸻

Design Traits

Use

* warm off-whites
* soft greys
* restrained colour palette
* subtle hand-drawn accents
* rounded corners
* generous whitespace
* editorial layouts
* clean typography
* simple illustrations

Avoid

* glossy gradients
* neon AI aesthetics
* crypto/cyberpunk styling
* sharp aggressive UI
* excessive animation
* clutter
* corporate stock photography
* abstract 3D blobs
* design with a political message

⸻

Illustration Style

Illustrations should:

* feel human
* be slightly imperfect
* use simple metaphors
* support understanding

Good examples:

* shields
* tangled threads
* filters
* compasses
* calming visual metaphors
* focus and clarity imagery

Avoid:

* futuristic robots
* anime styles
* sci-fi visuals

⸻

Product Philosophy

Better Net does not tell users what to think.

It helps users:

* notice manipulation
* reduce noise
* browse intentionally
* regain focus
* make informed choices

The browser should feel empowering, not paternalistic.

⸻

Messaging Themes

Core themes:

* “Your goals, not the feed”
* clarity over manipulation
* focus over distraction
* users over algorithms
* understanding over outrage
* transparency over coercion
